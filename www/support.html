Before contacting us

SynDiary cannot access or recover the data on your device. Make an export before troubleshooting storage, reinstalling, moving devices, or clearing app data. Keep recovery material private.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

In SynDiary app storage, encrypted at rest by default. There is no SynDiary account and no SynDiary backend that stores the data you keep in the app. On Android, Google backup or device transfer can include eligible app data when that operating-system service is enabled.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings in the app and use its export or backup flow. Keep the resulting file and recovery material in a safe place you control. Do not rely on an operating-system backup as your only copy.

Why does SynDiary ask for a permission?

Microphone access is used only when you record; photo access is used when you select an image; calendar access is used for local event import; and notifications are used for device-scheduled reminders. Android builds do not request broad audio or video-library access.

Does SynDiary send my data to AI providers?

Not by default. Supported AI routes can run on device. A cloud route is used only after you enable it and provide your own supported-provider key; the app shows when that route is active.

How do I report an unsafe AI response?

Use “Report response” beneath the displayed assistant response, choose a category, review the exact text, and confirm. Select or copy the reference from the receipt shown after submission. To request deletion before the scheduled deletion after 89 days, email that reference to info@syndiary.com.

How do I erase my data?

Use the erase controls in Settings or uninstall the app. Android backup copies are managed through your Google backup settings. Contact us separately if you previously sent support mail or an AI response report.

Contact

Email info@syndiary.com. Helpful details are your platform, app version, the action you took, and the visible error. Do not attach data exports, recovery codes, API keys, or sensitive entries unless support explicitly asks for a narrowly scoped, redacted sample.

For privacy and report-deletion requests, put “Privacy request” in the subject. Include the AI report reference when relevant.
