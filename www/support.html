SynDiary Support

Start with the user guide or troubleshooting guide. You can also email info@syndiary.com.

Before contacting us

SynDiary has no account and cannot access or recover the diary data on your device. Include the app version and platform when asking for help. Never send diary exports, recovery material, API keys, or sensitive entries.

Where is my data stored?

In SynDiary app storage on your device, encrypted at rest by default. SynDiary operates no diary-data storage or sync backend. On Android, eligible encrypted app data may be handled by Google backup or device transfer when that operating-system service is enabled.

How do I make a backup?

Use the export or backup controls in Settings. Keep the resulting file and recovery material somewhere safe that you control. Do not rely on an operating-system backup as your only copy.

Why does SynDiary ask for a permission?

Microphone access is used only when you record; photo access is used for images you attach or a user-initiated local photo import; calendar access is used for local event import; files are handled through system pickers; and notifications are used for device-scheduled reminders. Android builds do not request broad audio or video-library access.

Does SynDiary send my data to AI providers?

Not by default. Optional BYOK cloud AI is off by default. A cloud route is used only after you enable it, provide your own supported-provider key, and approve content for the selected provider. The app shows when that route is active.

How do I report an AI response?

Use “Report response,” select one allowed category, review the exact assistant response in the confirmation preview, and confirm. The report contains only that exact response, the category, a stable random AIR reference, and client submission time. Copy the AIR reference from the receipt.

Reports are deleted after at most 89 days. For earlier deletion, email only the AIR reference to info@syndiary.com. Do not email the reported response.

How do I erase my data?

Use the erase controls in Settings or uninstall the app. Android backup copies are managed through your Google backup settings. Contact us separately if you previously sent a website message, support email, or AI response report.
