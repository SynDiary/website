Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

SynDiary is a free, accountless, local-first personal data store with no ads or tracking. Your entries, imports, media, reports, on-device diagnostics, and sanitized crash records stay in app storage by default. SynDiary operates no backend that stores or syncs the personal data you keep in the app.

Data leaves only through the specific operating-system services and deliberate actions described below — including exports, optional BYOK cloud AI, and a confirmed report of an AI assistant response.

1. Who we are

SynDiary is developed by the SynDiary team (the "controller" for the limited processing described below, where any takes place). You can reach us at info@syndiary.com for any privacy question or request.

2. No account, no registration

The app works without an account. We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details when you use the app. The only time we receive personal data from you is when you choose to contact us (for example by email or through the contact form on this website).

3. What stays on your device

The following is processed in app storage by default:

  • Entries, notes, moods, events, people, places, reports, and anything else you record.
  • Archive, calendar, and selected photo data you deliberately import.
  • Photos, audio recordings, and other media you attach.
  • Settings, on-device diagnostics, and sanitized crash records.

SynDiary stores its database encrypted at rest by default. We cannot read or recover this data because no SynDiary server receives it. Android is an exception to the shorthand “only on this device”: when you enable Google backup or device transfer, eligible encrypted app data may be handled by that operating-system service under your Google settings and Google’s terms.

4. What can leave your device — and when

Each item below is controlled by you or by an operating-system setting. Nothing else is uploaded automatically.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share, you choose the destination. An encrypted-at-rest export is available; a plain-text export warns that the exported copy is unprotected. The receiving app, storage location, or person handles the copy under its own practices.

Cloud AI (optional, off by default, your own key)

SynDiary's AI features run on your device by default. If your device cannot run a local model, or you prefer a cloud model, you can connect your own API key for a supported provider: Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic for chat, and OpenAI or Deepgram for voice transcription.

Cloud AI stays off until you explicitly enable it and add a key. Only content you approve for that request is sent over HTTPS directly to your selected provider and processed under that provider's own terms, using your key. A notice and persistent indicator show when a cloud route is active. Turn cloud AI off at any time in Settings to return to on-device processing.

Provider policies: Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Deepgram. These providers are typically based in the United States, so choosing one means your submitted content is transferred there under your direction and your agreement with that provider.

AI assistant response reports (only after confirmation)

If the AI assistant produces a response you consider harmful, inaccurate, or inappropriate, you can report it so the SynDiary team can identify patterns and improve content-safety measures. This is the sole purpose of the feature; reports are never used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

Before anything is sent, SynDiary shows a confirmation preview containing the exact response and your selected category. A submitted report contains only these four fields:

  • the exact assistant response shown in the confirmation preview;
  • one allowed report category (e.g. "harmful", "inaccurate");
  • a stable random AIR-… report reference; and
  • the client submission time.

Nothing else is included. SynDiary never attaches your prompt, conversation history, app AI memories, API key, email, account, device identifier, or location.

The report is sent over HTTPS to the dedicated first-party https://www.syndiary.com/api/ai-output-reports service. The service accepts a strict, size-limited schema, limits submissions to six per 60 seconds per IP and domain, and stores identical retries only once under the stable AIR reference. Reports are kept in a dedicated store and are not shared with third parties or used for tracking.

Retention: every report is automatically deleted after at most 89 days. No action is needed on your part for this automatic deletion.

Earlier deletion on request: to delete a report before the 89-day window, email info@syndiary.com with the copyable AIR-… reference shown after submission. That reference is the only lookup key needed — no account or personal details are required to request deletion.

AI model downloads (user-initiated)

If you download an on-device AI model, the app fetches the model files from Hugging Face's content delivery network. This is a standard web download: it transmits no personal content, though the file host — like any website you visit — sees your IP address.

Calendar imports and feed connections (only if you add one)

Device-calendar imports and calendar-file imports are read locally and involve no network access. If you add a calendar feed by URL, the app periodically fetches that feed so its events appear in your timeline. The request goes directly from your device to the calendar host, which sees standard web-request metadata.

App update check (automatic, anonymous)

On mobile, the app periodically (at most every six hours) fetches a small static file from this website to learn whether the installed version is still safe to use. The request carries no identifiers, no cookies, and no personal data; like any web request, it reveals your IP address and the fact that the app launched to our web host.

On-device diagnostics and crash records

On-device diagnostics and sanitized crash records remain on your device. Nothing is uploaded automatically. If you choose “send feedback,” the app opens a message you can review and edit before sending.

Android backup and device transfer

Android may include eligible encrypted SynDiary app data in Google backup or device transfer when that service is enabled in your operating-system settings. Downloaded AI models and temporary staging data are excluded. Google controls transport, storage, retention, and restore under your account settings. This is operating-system-managed backup, not a SynDiary-operated sync backend.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Data on your device: processed locally under your sole control; it never reaches us, so we do not process it at all.
  • Cloud AI: your explicit consent and direction, given by enabling the feature and confirmed in-session (GDPR Article 6(1)(a); for any special-category content you choose to submit, Article 9(2)(a)). Withdraw at any time by turning cloud AI off.
  • AI assistant response reports: your explicit consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and, when the response contains special-category content, Article 9(2)(a). You can withdraw consent for a stored report through deletion by AIR reference.
  • Update check and website hosting logs: our legitimate interest in keeping released software safe and the website operating (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Support requests: handling the request you sent us (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)).

6. Retention and deletion

You control data held in the app: edit or delete entries, export a copy, erase local app data, or uninstall the app. Android backup copies are controlled through your Google settings.

AI assistant response reports are deleted after at most 89 days and can be deleted earlier using the AIR reference. Emails and website contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the request, subject to legal and security needs. Hosting providers may retain short-lived operational logs.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, and port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. For data in the app these rights are self-service: the app itself is your access, correction, deletion, and export tool, because only you hold the data. For anything involving us directly (for example support emails), contact info@syndiary.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

8. Permissions

SynDiary may ask for device permissions, always for a local purpose:

  • Microphone: to record voice notes. Transcription runs on your device by default; audio is sent to a cloud provider only if you enabled cloud voice transcription with your own key (see section 4).
  • Calendar: to import calendar events you select into your timeline, locally.
  • Photos: to attach images and run a user-initiated local photo import; SynDiary does not request broad Android audio or video-library access.
  • Files: through system pickers for archives and exports you choose.
  • Notifications: for reminders scheduled on the device.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings.

9. Age

SynDiary is intended for people aged 13 and over. It has no account-registration path. If you believe a minor submitted information through support, the website contact form, or an AI assistant response report, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

10. Service providers and transfers

Our website, contact form, AI-report API, and dedicated report store are hosted by Netlify. The website contact form uses reCAPTCHA. Optional cloud AI and network calendar feeds connect to the provider you select. These providers may process data outside your country under their own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date. For meaningful changes to what can leave your device, we will also update the in-app disclosures before the change takes effect.
