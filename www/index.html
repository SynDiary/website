Welcome to SynDiary:
Your Private, Local-First Diary

Keep entries, events, people, places, media, and imports in one private place. SynDiary is free, works without an account, and operates no backend that stores or syncs your diary data.

Get SynDiary
No SynDiary Account

Open the app and start capturing what matters without registration or a developer-held identity.

Local by Default

Entries, imports, media, diagnostics, and sanitized crash records stay in app storage on your device.

Portable and Yours

AI on Your Terms

About SynDiary

A Calm Place for
The Details of Your Life

Create personal entries, bring in selected calendar events and supported archives, attach photos or voice entries, and revisit everything in a chronological timeline.

Create and Revisit

Capture the moments and context you want to remember, then return to them through your timeline.

Benefits

Private by Default

SynDiary has no account system and no developer-operated backend that stores or syncs your diary data.

Timeline and Reports

Revisit entries in a chronological view and explore simple reports calculated on your device.

Deliberate Imports

Bring in supported archives, selected calendar data, and photos through user-controlled import flows.

AI, Your Choice

Keep supported AI work on device, or turn on optional BYOK cloud AI and approve content sent to your selected provider.

Back Up and Export

Create a portable copy, share an export, or erase local app data using controls in Settings.

Free, Without Ads

The SynDiary app is free and has no ads, tracking, or paid tier.
