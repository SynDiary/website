Your Data Stays With You by Default. Now Your AI Can Do More With It.

Smarter local AI, more responsive imports, smoother timelines, and more control over your personal data.

02.11.2026·The SynDiary Team

Your personal data tells a story.

Calendar events, memories, notes, photos, and information gathered over many years. But as that collection grows, finding what matters and making sense of it should become easier, not more complicated.

That's what we're working towards with SynDiary.

SynDiary 1.0.2 focuses on four things: making local AI more useful, improving performance, simplifying imports, and giving you better tools to organise your information.

And we're doing it while staying true to our philosophy: Your data, your rules.

Local AI: Your personal information, your device

AI becomes more useful when it understands context.

But should you have to send your personal history to a remote server every time you want to ask a question about it?

We believe you should have a choice.

SynDiary already supports AI models that run directly on compatible smartphones. Your personal information can be processed locally, without having to upload it to a cloud AI service. Your information stays on your device by default; optional cloud AI, sharing, reporting and operating-system backups can involve external services.

With this update, we're expanding the selection of local AI models and improving the way they work with your personal information.

SynDiary uses device compatibility and memory requirements to decide which newer AI models to offer, and checks available storage before installation. This makes it easier to choose a suitable option.

We've also improved how local AI understands your questions.

It now focuses more closely on what you've just asked, finds more relevant information from your entries, and better distinguishes your questions from earlier AI responses.

The result we're aiming for is simple: more relevant answers, less waiting, and an AI assistant that works with your own information.

We've also improved how interrupted responses are handled. You can stop an answer without losing the text already generated, and the app makes it clearer when a response or summary is incomplete.

More AI choices for different devices

Not every smartphone has the same capabilities.

That's why SynDiary offers different local AI models depending on the memory and performance available on your device.

The updated selection includes compact options for compatible older iPhones and more capable models for selected higher-memory iPhones and Android phones.

On iPhone, supported choices include Qwen and LFM2, plus Gemma on eligible devices. Android currently supports Gemma on eligible higher-memory devices; lower-memory Android phones do not have a local model option in this release.

We're continuing to test additional AI models and processing engines for future releases.

Our goal isn't to make everyone use the largest AI model. It's to make useful, private AI accessible on more devices.

Your timeline, with less waiting

A personal data collection becomes more valuable over time.

But what happens when it contains tens of thousands of entries?

We've improved SynDiary's timeline so it opens faster, scrolls more smoothly, and reduces waiting when browsing older information.

Whether you're looking back at last week's events or exploring memories from years ago, navigating your history should feel natural.

Because your personal history shouldn't become harder to explore simply because there's more of it.

Bring your Facebook and Instagram history together, more smoothly

Your digital history doesn't live in one place.

Some memories are in your calendar. Others are in Facebook or Instagram archives, or in information you've collected over the years.

SynDiary helps bring that information into a structured personal timeline.

We've improved the performance and responsiveness of Facebook and Instagram archive imports.

You can now continue using other parts of SynDiary while an import runs, with visible progress and an in-app message when it finishes. System notifications depend on permissions and platform support. On iOS, imports pause when the app is suspended until you return.

Less waiting. More time to explore what you've brought together.

More control over what you keep

Having control over your personal data also means being able to organise and remove what you no longer need.

SynDiary now supports selecting and deleting multiple entries at once.

Instead of removing entries individually, you can tidy up a larger collection in fewer steps.

It's a practical improvement, but an important one.

After all, control over your information includes the freedom to decide what stays and what goes.

A private personal data hub that grows with you

We don't see SynDiary as simply another notes app.

We're building a private personal data hub where information from different parts of your life can come together, become connected, and remain useful over time.

Local AI, more responsive imports, better performance, and easier organisation are all steps in that direction.

There's still much more we want to build.

But our starting point remains the same:

Your data - your rules.

SynDiary is available on iOS and Android, with a free Core version.

Try SynDiary: https://www.syndiary.com

And we'd love to hear your thoughts: What would you want a private AI assistant to help you discover in your own personal history?
