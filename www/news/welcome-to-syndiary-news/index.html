Welcome to
SynDiary News

1 October 2026·The SynDiary Team

A few weeks after our launch, we’re opening this News section to stay in touch with you - our first SynDiary users.

Here, we’ll share app updates, practical guides, and news about what’s coming next. We’ll publish updates on our website first, then share them on our social media channels so you can follow along wherever you prefer.

For our first post, we’d like to introduce SynDiary Help, our dedicated documentation website. It’s a place to find guidance on getting started, using the app, managing your data, and understanding privacy and security.

For example, our step-by-step Facebook and Instagram guide shows you how to export your information from Meta and download it to your device. Direct import into SynDiary is coming soon; for now, the guide helps you prepare your archive and keep it safely stored until that option is available.

Thank you for being here from the beginning. Your questions and feedback will help us make SynDiary and the guidance around it more useful to you.

The SynDiary Team
