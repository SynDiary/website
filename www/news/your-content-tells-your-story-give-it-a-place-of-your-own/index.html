Stories

Your content tells your story. Give it a place of your own.

Bring supported Facebook and Instagram posts into your personal timeline, add your own reflections, and revisit the work you have shared.

16.11.2026·The SynDiary Team

A journey you shared. An interview you published. When you create and share online, you leave a record of your work and your life. Over time, those posts show how your interests and your voice have changed.

SynDiary gives you a place to collect supported Facebook and Instagram posts and add the context you want to remember.

Revisit the work you've shared

For creators, influencers and media professionals, published content can hold years of effort and experience.

An old travel post might remind you why you started sharing. A project announcement might bring back the work and people behind it. You may find an idea you want to develop further, or simply enjoy seeing how far you've come.

Bring your posts into your personal timeline

You can download your Facebook or Instagram archive and import it into SynDiary. Supported posts appear as social media entries in your timeline, alongside entries you create yourself.

Not every item in a Meta archive is supported, and some media may be skipped. Keep the original archive as a separate copy and review the import results.

Add a reflection about an experience or write down a thought you want to keep. Your own entries can capture details you left out of the public post.

To return to a post, search for a word from its text. Open Filter by Type, leave only Social Media selected, and tap Apply Filters to focus on imported posts.

Keep your collection on your device

SynDiary processes your Meta import on your device and stores your imported history locally by default. The SynDiary team does not receive your archive.

Some optional features can send information off your device. Our privacy guide explains which features do this and when.

Your collection can grow with the notes and experiences you choose to add.

Start with a piece of your story

Make room on your device for both the downloaded ZIP and the imported content. Request your archive and allow time for Meta to prepare it. Large imports also take time to process on your device.

Our Facebook and Instagram download guide explains how to request your archive and select the ZIP in SynDiary.

Enter your author name for Facebook or your username for Instagram, review the import options, and tap Start Import. Both import screens initially select the past year, so move the start date back before importing older posts.

Then look for something you remember creating or sharing. Write down what it means to you now.

Get SynDiary and start your personal collection.
