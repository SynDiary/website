SynDiary is a local-first personal data store: one private place for the data your life produces, from notes and events to people, places, media, and imports from other platforms. There's no account and no registration. Everything lives in an encrypted database on your device, and the SynDiary team runs no backend that stores the data you keep in the app. Specific user-controlled or operating-system-managed routes can send data elsewhere; see [Privacy, security & permissions](/privacy-security-permissions/) for details.

## Get the app

SynDiary is currently in public beta:

- **Android:** join through [Google Play](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.syndiary.coreapp).
- **iOS:** join through [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/mf47MN6d).

## First launch

The first time you open SynDiary, a short set of intro slides gives you a quick sense of what the app does. After that, you'll go through storage setup, where SynDiary creates the encrypted database that holds everything you keep in the app.

If your encrypted storage uses a recovery code, storage setup is where you'll get it. Keep it somewhere safe, like a password manager or another secure place you won't lose. Your data is encrypted on your device and never sent anywhere, so that code is the only way back in if you're ever locked out. Without it, nobody can recover that data, not even the SynDiary team.

## Finding your way around

SynDiary's main screen has four tabs along the bottom, with a central button for adding new entries:

- **Timeline:** your entries and imported events, grouped by date.
- **Reports:** stats and charts drawn from what you've logged.
- **+ (center):** start a new entry, of any type.
- **AI Chat:** talk with an assistant about your data.
- **Settings:** configure the app, including AI, privacy, and advanced options.
